## Map data and rendering

Mapbox GL JS is used when a public Mapbox token is configured, under Mapbox’s terms. MapLibre GL JS and OpenFreeMap provide the keyless development map. Renderer attribution controls remain visible. The campus building centers were checked against OpenStreetMap; OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License.
